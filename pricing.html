Is $119.99 a problem?

The annual fee has to replace friction, not merely add features.

For an occasional owner with a short checklist, Anchor may be enough—and it is designed to stay enough. Cruiser is for the owner managing several systems, a meaningful spares inventory, provisioning, regular passages or another person who needs the same vessel record.

The price works out to roughly $10 per month, but it is billed annually. The value case is not “another weather app.” It is one durable record that helps prevent missed service, duplicate parts purchases, lost receipts and incomplete trip history.

Why annual-only?

Boat ownership is seasonal, but the vessel record is not. Maintenance history, winter work, documents and inventory remain valuable through haul-out. Annual billing matches that continuity and avoids a cycle where a logbook disappears from the workflow every autumn.

BoatWorks Pro is technically freemium: free download + permanent free functionality + optional subscription. “Freemium” does not mean a short free trial or a paid app with locked screens.
Pricing FAQ

Clear before you subscribe.

Will the free tier expire?

No. Anchor is intended to remain a permanent free plan.

Can I pay monthly?

No. Cruiser is annual-only at $119.99 per year.

Is there still an up-front app purchase?

No. The download purchase price was removed. BoatWorks Pro is a free download with subscriptions inside the app.

What happens to my records if I stop subscribing?

Your records are not deleted. Features above the free allowance may become read-only or limited, while the underlying vessel history remains safe.

Does every crew member pay?

No. Cruiser’s sharing model is designed so the vessel owner covers the shared boat; invited crew do not need a separate subscription for that shared record.
