Privacy is a product feature

No account to create. No company cloud holding your boat.

Your vessel records live on your device and, when enabled, sync through your own private iCloud account. BoatWorks Pro has no server that receives your maintenance records, inventory, routes or documents.

That architecture also means BoatWorks Pro does not impose an artificial 1 GB document allowance. Capacity is determined by the storage available on your device and in your iCloud account.

Common questions

Before you bring the boat aboard.

Is BoatWorks Pro free?

Yes. BoatWorks Pro will be free to download, and Anchor is a permanent free tier—not a timed trial. Cruiser is an optional $119.99 annual subscription.

Does BoatWorks Pro limit document storage?

BoatWorks Pro does not impose a document count or gigabyte quota. Files are stored on your device and, when iCloud is enabled, in your own iCloud account, so available device and iCloud capacity are the practical limits.

Can I use it without cell service?

Core vessel records are stored locally and remain available without an internet connection. Live weather, alerts, map tiles and new cloud synchronization need connectivity; nearby device-to-device crew sync is designed for local use.

Is $119.99 charged monthly?

No. Cruiser is annual-only: $119.99 per year, which works out to about $10 per month when averaged across the year. There is no monthly plan.

Does it support NMEA 2000 or Signal K?

That connected-boat capability belongs to the coming Commodore tier and is not being sold as part of launch-day Cruiser.
