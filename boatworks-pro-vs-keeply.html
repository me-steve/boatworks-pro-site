BoatWorks Pro vs Keeply

Two boat apps. Two very different ideas of where your boat belongs.

Keeply leads with fast AI-assisted setup and a hosted cross-platform account. BoatWorks Pro is a deeper, Apple-native vessel system built around local records, private iCloud and onboard operations.

Where BoatWorks Pro is meaningfully different

1. Storage is yours, not an allowance from us

Keeply’s paid plan advertises 1 GB of document storage. BoatWorks Pro does not place an app-level quota on manuals, invoices, certificates or photos. Your device and iCloud capacity are the real limits.

2. The trip log can record the route automatically

Keeply supports passage and watch entries. BoatWorks Pro Cruiser adds phone-GPS breadcrumbs so the route builds while you operate the boat, then keeps maps and operating context with the log.

3. Inventory means finding the part

BoatWorks Pro goes beyond a list: quantities, minimum levels and a zone/location/container hierarchy answer whether the spare exists and which locker or bin holds it.

4. Fuel, provisions and conditions are first-class

Dedicated tanks and fuel events, food and consumables with expiry, tide curves and marine-relevant weather are part of one vessel workflow rather than separate apps.

5. Privacy is structural

There is no BoatWorks account or hosted boat database. Core records stay on Apple devices and in the owner’s private iCloud. BoatWorks Pro does not send core records to an AI provider.

Where Keeply may be the better fit

Keeply’s AI-guided setup, photo identification, document reading and parts recommendations can reduce initial data entry. It also supports web and Android, offers monthly billing, and publicly lists unlimited maintenance on its free tier. Those are real advantages for owners who prioritize quick setup, cross-platform access or a lower short-term commitment.

This page is intended to help owners choose, not to pretend every difference favors us. Keeply and BoatWorks Pro both support offline access to core records and both offer crew-sharing features.

Which should you choose?

Choose Keeply if AI setup, web/Android access and monthly pricing matter most. Choose BoatWorks Pro if you use iPhone/iPad and want a private, connected vessel record with GPS routes, structured spares and provisions, fuel tanks, tides, weather and no app-imposed document quota.
