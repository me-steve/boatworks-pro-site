Quick answers

How do I play?

Guide each plane from the runway to its gate and back out for departure. When a plane reaches an intersection, choose which way it should turn.

How do I unlock new aircraft?

Earn coins by completing levels, then spend them to unlock bigger aircraft. The largest planes also need stars.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device. Deleting the app removes your saved progress, and progress doesn’t transfer between devices.

Something looks wrong in a level

Email us with the level name and a screenshot if you can. It helps us fix it quickly.

Still need a hand?

Email crew@boatworks-pro.com. Include your device model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened.
