Privacy Policy

Your progress stays on your device.

Taxiway Tycoon doesn’t need an account and doesn’t send your gameplay anywhere.

Effective September 25, 2026

The short version

Taxiway Tycoon does not require an account, does not ask for your name or email, and does not collect or transmit personal information. Your game progress is stored only on your device.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your game progress locally, including levels completed, stars, coins, and aircraft you’ve unlocked, so you can pick up where you left off. This information is not sent to us, and we can’t see it.

Information we don’t request

We don’t ask for your location, contacts, photos, or any other personal data, and the app does not track you across other apps or websites.

Your choices

Deleting Taxiway Tycoon from your device removes all of its saved progress.

Children’s privacy

Taxiway Tycoon is a general-audience game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or legal requirements change. The effective date above shows when the latest version took effect.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email crew@boatworks-pro.com with “Taxiway Tycoon Privacy” in the subject.
