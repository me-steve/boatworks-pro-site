Complete boat management

One app for the work, records and rhythms of owning a boat.

BoatWorks Pro connects the parts of vessel ownership that generic task managers, spreadsheets and paper logbooks keep apart.

Know what is due

Maintenance & machinery

Build the schedule around the equipment aboard.

Create recurring and one-time work, attach documentation and see what needs service without flattening the boat into one generic checklist.

  • Calendar and usage-based maintenance intervals.
  • Separate records for engines, transmissions, generators, pumps and systems.
  • Completed service history, notes, photos and attachments.
  • Status that surfaces overdue and upcoming work.
Deep dive: boat maintenance →
Fuel management

Make every gallon part of the boat’s history.

Record fill-ups, transfers, tank levels, fuel used, price and cost. BoatWorks Pro keeps fuel information with the vessel instead of leaving it across dock receipts and notes.

  • Multiple fuel tanks and fill history.
  • Quantity, cost and average price visibility.
  • Usage records that add context to passages.
  • A foundation for connected instrument data in Commodore.
Inventory & provisions

Know what is aboard and precisely where it lives.

Inventory handles parts and consumables; Provisions gives food, drinks and galley supplies their own workflow. Both are designed for the nested reality of a boat.

  • Zone, location and container hierarchy.
  • Quantities, minimum levels and low-stock signals.
  • Barcode-assisted entry and drawdown.
  • Expiry dates and storage locations for provisions.
Deep dive: inventory and provisions →
Tides, weather & alerts

Conditions belong beside the boat plan.

View the tide station nearest the boat, today’s highs and lows, detailed curves, forecasts and official weather alerts without rebuilding your vessel context in another app.

  • Free daily tide highs and lows.
  • Cruiser tide curves, day stepping and station selection.
  • Marine-relevant WeatherKit forecast detail.
  • National Weather Service alerts where available.
