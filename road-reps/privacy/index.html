01

The short version

Road Reps does not require an account, ask for your name, or upload your workout activity. Your rep multiplier is saved on your device. The app displays ads through Google Mobile Ads, which may process device and usage information as described below.

♠No accountNo sign-up or login

02

Information and how it’s used

Information stored by Road Reps

The app saves your selected repetition multiplier (1×, 1.5×, or 2×) in local device storage so your preference is ready next time. The current deck and completed-card count exist only while you use the app and reset when the app session ends. We do not receive this information.

Information Road Reps does not request

We do not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, payment information, or fitness and health records. Road Reps does not connect to Apple Health or require an account.

03

Advertising and third parties

Road Reps uses Google Mobile Ads to display banner advertising. Depending on your region, device settings, and consent choices, Google and its advertising partners may collect or process information such as device identifiers, IP address, ad interactions, and diagnostic data to provide, measure, secure, or personalize ads.

i

Google handles that information under its own policies. Review Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Road Reps uses Google’s consent tools where required. We do not sell your personal information ourselves, and we do not receive a list of individual users from Google.

04

Your choices

  • Advertising consentWhere required, use “Privacy choices” inside Road Reps to review or change your selections.
  • Device controlsYou can manage tracking permissions and personalized-ad settings in your device settings.
  • Local preferenceRemoving the app deletes the repetition multiplier stored by Road Reps on that device.

05

Other details

Children’s privacy

Road Reps is a general-audience fitness app and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Security and retention

Road Reps does not operate an account database or server for your workout activity. Information processed by Google is retained and protected under Google’s policies and your applicable consent choices.

Policy updates

We may update this policy when the app or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top will show when the latest version took effect.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the developer through the support link on the Road Reps App Store listing. Include “Road Reps Privacy” in your message.
