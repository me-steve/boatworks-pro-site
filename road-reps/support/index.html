Quick answers

Most Road Reps questions can be solved in a few taps.

♠

How does a workout work?

Draw a card. Its rank selects the exercise, while its suit sets the base repetitions. Complete the exercise, then draw again until the deck is finished.

♥

What does the multiplier do?

Choose 1×, 1.5×, or 2× before drawing your first card. Road Reps applies it to each card’s base reps and remembers the choice on your device.

♣

How do I start over?

Tap Reset in the top-right corner during a workout. After the final card, choose Start a New Deck to shuffle all 52 cards again.

♦

Where are the full rules?

Tap How to play in the top-left corner of Road Reps. You’ll see the suit values and all 13 rank-to-exercise assignments.

♠

How do I change ad privacy choices?

Where required, open How to play and select Privacy choices. Your device settings also provide controls for tracking and personalized ads.

♥

Does Road Reps save workout history?

No. Your current deck lasts only for the app session. Road Reps stores only your selected repetition multiplier locally on your device.
