Where BoatWorks Pro is meaningfully different

1. Built from the ground up to work offline

BoatWorks Pro is a local-first app: your vessel record lives on the device in your hand, not behind a network call to a hosted account. Everything core works at the fuel dock, in a dead marina Wi-Fi zone or offshore with no signal at all — sync to your own private iCloud simply catches up when a connection returns.

2. Storage is yours, not an allowance from anyone

Keeply caps paid storage at 1 GB; TheBoatApp meters 300 MB to 10 GB per boat by tier; Ready4Sea doesn't publish a number. BoatWorks Pro does not place an app-level quota on manuals, invoices, certificates or photos — your device and iCloud capacity are the real limits.

3. The trip log can record the route automatically

All three competitors rely on entries you type by hand. BoatWorks Pro Cruiser adds phone-GPS breadcrumbs so the route builds while you operate the boat, then keeps maps and operating context with the log.

4. Inventory means finding the part

BoatWorks Pro goes beyond a general equipment list: quantities, minimum levels and a zone/location/container hierarchy answer whether the spare exists and which locker or bin holds it.

5. Fuel, provisions and conditions are first-class

Dedicated tanks and fuel events, food and consumables with expiry, tide curves and marine-relevant weather are part of one vessel workflow rather than a general cost-tracking page or a separate app.

6. Multiple boats, still private

BoatWorks Pro supports more than one vessel without a hosted account or a per-boat storage license — each boat's records stay local-first and in your own private iCloud.

The short version

Every row above is a capability BoatWorks Pro ships today: no hosted account, no app-imposed storage quota, automatic GPS trip logging, structured spares and provisions, dedicated fuel tanks, tides and marine weather, and support for more than one vessel — all without sending your records to a third party. That's the whole product, not a trial funnel.
