Boat maintenance app

Stop maintaining “the boat.” Maintain every system aboard.

A useful maintenance log connects each job to the engine, generator, pump, sail drive or safety system it serves—and preserves the evidence after the wrench is put away.

A schedule built for date and usage

Some work is due every spring. Some is due every 100 engine hours. BoatWorks Pro supports recurring work and one-time jobs so routine service, haul-out projects and that nagging repair do not compete in one undifferentiated list.

  • Calendar-based recurring maintenance.
  • Machinery-linked service and remaining-life status.
  • One-time tasks for repairs and projects.
  • Completed history that stays with the asset.

Why equipment context matters

“Change oil” is not enough on a boat with two engines, a generator and a transmission. Each asset needs its own specifications, attachments, hours and work history. That context makes the record useful to the owner doing the work, the technician diagnosing a problem and the future buyer evaluating care.

Attach the proof to the work

Keep service photos, invoices, manuals and notes with the relevant record. BoatWorks Pro does not impose a separate document quota; available device and private iCloud capacity are the practical limits.

See the state of the vessel

The Service Manifest separates work requiring attention from systems that remain on track. The dashboard turns a collection of tasks into an operational answer: what needs action before the next trip?

A maintenance history is a vessel asset

A complete record can reduce repeated diagnosis, make seasonal handoffs easier and support the story of a well-cared-for boat. BoatWorks Pro keeps current work and completed work together instead of archiving the useful history out of sight.

Anchor includes a practical free maintenance allowance. Cruiser removes the core task and equipment caps for owners managing the whole boat.
